# Specimen

The dataset contains mixed TEM specimens from multiple biological sources, including:

- **Rodent tissues:** rat heart, mouse liver-related samples, optic nerve/retina, and mouse immune-related samples.
- **Mammalian cell lines:** mainly human-derived cell lines, including A549, MCF-7, AGS, and RKO, with additional mouse cell samples.
- **Bacteria:** bacterial specimens, including *ST11* and *ST11KOrmpA*, as well as other bacterial morphology/infection sections.
- **Materials/other samples:** non-biological or mixed experimental samples such as PS-NH2 nanoparticles and numbered specimen blocks.
